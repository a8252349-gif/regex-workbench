# Content quality audit

**Verification date:** 2026-07-01

The content registry produces 120 localized core pages and 90 localized guide detail pages. Together with the language-neutral root page, the canonical sitemap contains 211 URLs.

## Inventory

- Six complete locale trees: English, Korean, Japanese, Spanish, French, and German
- Fifteen guide subjects per locale
- Ninety localized guide detail pages
- Localized home, tool, resource, trust, contact, policy, security, and methodology pages
- Unique localized titles and descriptions
- Real interactive tools on the matching search-intent pages
- No flag-name, country-name, or engine-name doorway-page expansion
- No fabricated reviews, ratings, user counts, awards, or download totals
- No Lorem ipsum, TODO, TBD, empty translation value, or production English fallback
- Fifteen primary guide patterns compiled and checked against designated success and rejection examples
- Exact-paragraph and five-word phrase comparison within each Latin-script locale; continuous character shingles for Korean and Japanese
- Known UI-copy and likely English-sentence regression scans over generated non-English HTML

## Enforced minimums

- Home: at least 1,200 words for English, Spanish, French, and German; 3,500 non-space Korean characters; 3,000 non-space Japanese characters
- Main tool pages: at least 700 words for Western-language pages; 2,000 Korean characters; 1,800 Japanese characters
- Guides: at least 1,000 words for English, Spanish, French, and German; 3,000 Korean characters; 2,800 Japanese characters

All configured thresholds passed in the final static build. The highest measured localized-page phrase similarity was **42.0%**, between the English About and Editorial Policy pages, below the configured build-failure threshold. Guide topics use different concept sets, examples, error cases, performance concerns, and FAQs rather than keyword substitutions over one article shell.

Automated similarity is not proof of perfect originality or editorial quality. It is a regression barrier for repeated paragraphs and excessive phrase overlap. A human launch review should still check factual nuance, natural translation, visual rhythm, and how each page answers its intended search query.
